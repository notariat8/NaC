# Notarkammer Demo: One-Page Handout and Talk Track

Date: 2026-06-22

## Goal

The meeting should position NaC as a 100% notariat-ready ISV candidate. The
concrete goal is XNP/SNP test access, clarifiable pilot roles, and the next
step toward ISV listing. The demo does not claim productive access; it creates
a safe discussion frame for approval, test data, evidence fields, status
callbacks, and certification requirements.

## Primary Proceeding

The primary proceeding is the real estate purchase agreement. It is concrete
enough to show drafting, notarization, priority notice, right of first refusal,
tax clearance certificate, deletion documents, purchase-price maturity, and
closing in one BPMN picture.

## BPMN Talk Track

- NaC shows the real estate purchase agreement as a BPMN proceeding with
  parallel closing paths, duration bands, critical path, and evidence gates.
- XNP remains the local notarial workspace; XNotar stands for land register
  and register preparation, validation, signature, and beN dispatch.
- The card reader, SAK/KMC, and signature path are shown only as a local
  readiness boundary; NaC stores no PINs, card values, or tokens.
- Register and land register appear as external waiting, proof, and feedback
  points, not as direct NaC data sources.
- Closing is explained as a professional gate chain: only when external
  evidence is present can the next BPMN step be released.

## Clear Non-Claims

- no productive XNP action,
- no matter data,
- no real register or land-register queries,
- no OCI actions,
- no secrets,
- no live calls,
- no claim that NaC productively controls XNP, XNotar, registers, or the land
  register.

## Closing Question

Which XNP/SNP test environment, ISV role, evidence fields, status callbacks,
error classes, and certification steps does NaC need so the real estate
purchase agreement can be prepared as an official ISV pilot?
